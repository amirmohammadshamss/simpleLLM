# TinyStories data-use record

Reviewed November 4, 2026. The [TinyStories dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories) identifies CDLA-Sharing-1.0. The source used here is Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li's original `TinyStories-train.txt` at pinned revision `f54c09fd23315a6f9c86f9dc80f725de7d8f9c64`.

The [license text](https://cdla.dev/sharing-1-0/) permits data use and publication subject to its conditions. Sections 3.1–3.3 require published data/enhanced data to retain the agreement, existing attribution, and notices identifying changes. Section 3.5 addresses computational results; its definition excludes outputs containing more than a de minimis portion of source data. This record supports local research use and does not classify every possible future model/output publication.

Project handling: raw stories, prepared arrays, and checkpoints remain local and gitignored. Metadata records source attribution, source/file hashes, selection, and transformations. Any future dataset export must preserve the agreement and attribution and identify modifications. A model/output release must separately consider source reproduction.

Attribution: **TinyStories — Ronen Eldan and Yuanzhi Li**, [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/roneneldan/TinyStories), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.07759). No pretrained model weights or pretrained tokenizer were downloaded.
